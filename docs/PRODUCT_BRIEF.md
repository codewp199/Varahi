# Product Brief — Working Draft

**Status:** Discovery  
**Venture:** Four-founder, service-led B2B web design and development startup  
**Initial geography:** Peoria-area independent businesses (hypothesis to validate)  
**Company name:** Varahi is a working project name; public brand is not yet confirmed.

## Objective

Test whether independent local businesses have an urgent, well-defined digital problem that our four-person team can solve reliably and profitably. The first offer should be narrow enough to deliver consistently, with clear scope, price, and support expectations.

## Customer hypothesis

Potential early customers may include independent retailers, grocery and specialty food stores, restaurants, home services, and other appointment- or inquiry-based businesses.

This is a list of segments to investigate, not a decision that all are target customers. Select an initial segment based on evidence from owner interviews and willingness to pay.

## Problem hypotheses to validate

- Business information is difficult to find or outdated online.
- A website is missing, slow, hard to use on mobile, or difficult for the owner to update.
- Customers rely on calls or social messages for information that could be answered online.
- Menu, service, location, hours, booking, or contact details are not presented clearly.
- The owner wants online ordering, booking, or lead capture but is unsure what solution fits.

Do not assume a business needs an app, AI, online ordering, or a custom integration until discovery confirms the need and operational readiness.

## Candidate initial offer

A fixed-scope, mobile-friendly business website with:

- Clear home, services/products, about, and contact information
- Click-to-call, map, and inquiry/contact paths
- Basic search metadata and accessibility checks
- Owner handoff and a short training session
- Optional, separately scoped maintenance

Ordering, payments, booking systems, inventory sync, custom dashboards, and AI features should be treated as optional follow-on work only after feasibility, cost, and customer demand are established.

## Value hypothesis

We can give a local owner a clear, maintainable online presence that helps customers understand what the business offers and how to take the next step. Any claim of increased revenue, traffic, or conversion must be supported by measured customer data before it appears in marketing.

## Evidence required before committing

For each segment, record:

- Number of relevant businesses identified
- Number contacted and interviewed
- Repeated problems and current workarounds
- Consequence and urgency of the problem
- Current spend or budget range
- Decision maker and buying process
- Existing tools and integrations
- Interest in a paid pilot, with exact requested scope and price

## MVP success criteria

The first validation milestone is reached when the team can:

1. Name one priority customer segment and explain why evidence supports it.
2. Describe one repeated problem in the customer's own words.
3. Present one fixed-scope offer with delivery effort, exclusions, price hypothesis, and support terms.
4. Secure a concrete next step from qualified prospects, ideally a paid pilot or a scheduled proposal review.

## Open decisions

- Which customer segment should we serve first?
- What one problem can we solve better and more simply than the customer's current workaround?
- What is included in the first package, and what is explicitly out of scope?
- Who owns sales, delivery, design/content, quality, and ongoing support?
- Which brand name and legal structure will be used publicly?
