# Founder Operating Rhythm

Designed for a four-founder team balancing the venture with other jobs. Meetings should produce decisions, owners, and dated deliverables.

## Weekly cadence

### Wednesday — 30-minute execution check-in

**Purpose:** Set a small number of outcome-based commitments for the rest of the week.

1. Review last week's commitments: Done / Not done / Blocked (5 min)
2. Review evidence or work completed since Sunday (5 min)
3. Agree on one company-level weekly outcome (5 min)
4. Each founder states one deliverable, owner, and due date (10 min)
5. Name blockers and confirm next check-in (5 min)

Bring updates in writing before the call. Do not use meeting time for a readout of work everyone can review asynchronously.

### Sunday — 45-minute review and presentation

**Purpose:** Show completed work, learn from evidence, and make only decisions needed for the next week.

1. Demonstrate deliverables or present findings (20 min)
2. Review customer feedback, quality, effort, and risks (10 min)
3. Record decisions and unresolved questions (10 min)
4. Preview next week's proposed outcome and owners (5 min)

Do not add new agenda items during the meeting unless they are urgent and need a decision.

## Weekly commitment format

A commitment is a verifiable output, not an activity.

**Weak:** “Research local businesses.”  
**Strong:** “Interview five independent retailers and upload five completed interview records by Sunday.”

Every commitment has:
- One directly responsible owner
- A measurable output
- A due date
- A definition of done
- A blocker or dependency, if any

Keep the company to one or two priority outcomes per week. Split work into tasks that fit each founder's actual available hours.

## Initial responsibility lanes

These are working lanes, not fixed titles. Founders can carry multiple lanes; assign one accountable owner per deliverable.

| Lane | Accountabilities |
|---|---|
| Product & Engineering | Customer problem translation, technical feasibility, architecture, website implementation, quality and delivery estimates |
| Customer Discovery & Sales | Prospect list, outreach, interviews, follow-ups, proposal pipeline and customer communication |
| Design & Content | Brand exploration, information architecture, visual direction, copy, and concept portfolio content |
| Operations & Delivery | Meeting notes, commitments, scope tracking, client onboarding, pricing/cost assumptions, and delivery process |

Reassign lanes based on demonstrated strengths and capacity. Do not leave an outcome jointly owned by all four.
